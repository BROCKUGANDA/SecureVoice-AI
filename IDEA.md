SecureVoice.ai is a privacy-first voice assistant that transcribes and answers conversations in real time without storing raw audio or exposing content to third-party models.

- Provide end-to-end encrypted voice calls, transcription, and responses.
- Generate actionable summaries, reminders, and follow-up tasks.
- Integrate with calendars, CRMs, and support tools through scoped connectors.
- Give users configurable retention, deletion, and access controls.
- Deliver tamper-evident audit logs and admin security analytics.

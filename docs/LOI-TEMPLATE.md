# Letter of Intent — template

> **How to use this.** Send this as a `.docx` or PDF with their letterhead
> already on it. The only things you should have to change are the bracketed
> fields and the signature block. Never ask them to draft it — friction is
> what kills a conversion, and a template that needs editing is a template that
> does not get signed.
>
> **Then get permission to name them.** Add one line to the covering email:
> *"May we name [Institution] and quote this letter in our submission and
> demo materials?"* Get the reply in writing, in the same thread. Without it,
> do not name them anywhere — see `docs/POST-LAUNCH-TODO.md`.

The parties wish to record their mutual intention to evaluate a pilot of
SecureVoice's real-time voice fraud-intervention service (the "Service"). This
letter records that intent. **It creates no legal obligation on either party,
no commitment of funding, and no exclusivity.**

### 4. What Phase 0 requires from the Institution

- Pseudonymised or mirrored alert data only (alert identifier, timestamp,
  amount, merchant, risk score).
- No account numbers, no card numbers, no customer contact details, no access
  to any internal system.
- SecureVoice places no calls and takes no customer action in Phase 0.

### 5. What SecureVoice provides at Phase 0

- The intervention decision the platform would have taken for each alert.
- The script it would have spoken.
- A readout quantifying, against the Institution's own historical cases, which
  fraud attempts would have been contained.

### 6. Data handling

SecureVoice processes data as a processor on the Institution's documented
instructions. Data minimisation, retention limits, sub-processors and deletion on
request are set out in the pilot brief and the data processing agreement, which
will be executed before Phase 1. SecureVoice registers as a data processor in
the pilot jurisdiction before Phase 1 begins.

### 7. Costs

Phase 0 is provided at no cost. Any commercial arrangement for later phases
would be the subject of a separate master services agreement and data
processing agreement.

### 8. Non-binding

Except for the parties' agreement on confidentiality and on the use of the
other's name in announcements, **this letter is non-binding** and creates no
obligation to proceed to any subsequent phase. Either party may terminate this
arrangement at any time without liability.

## Fallback ladder (SP-2)

If legal blocks even this non-binding letter, in descending order of strength:

1. Signed email from the named executive confirming intent to pilot.
2. Email from the sponsor with a role-attributed quote: *"Head of Fraud at a
   Ugandan tier-2 bank: 'the hardest part is reaching the customer in the first
   ninety seconds.'"* — with written permission.
3. A discovery call recorded in the submission at **L1** ("in active
   discussion"), with permission to name.

An honest L2 beats a dressed-up L1. Do not inflate a level.

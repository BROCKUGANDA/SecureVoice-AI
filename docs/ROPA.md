## 0. How to read this document

Every factual cell in this RoPA carries its own evidence. Three kinds of evidence appear, and they
are deliberately distinguishable:

| Marker      | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Example                                                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `file:line` | Read from this repository at the commit this document was written against. Re-openable in seconds.                                                                                                                  | `src/lib/privacy/retention.ts:44`                        |
| `measured`  | Observed against the running deployment or a live external system on 2026-10-18. Secrets were read as **length or presence only**; no key value is transcribed anywhere in this document.                           | Supabase pooler host in the production connection string |
| `OPEN`      | Not provable from the repository or the runtime. This is a real gap in the record, not a placeholder — an item the reviewer should expect to be asked about and should expect _not_ to be answered from code alone. | DPO designation                                          |

Why the discipline: a RoPA that asserts "data is encrypted at rest" because a marketing page says
so is worse than no RoPA. It becomes the document a regulator relies on, and then the first thing a
subpoena or an incident disproves. Article 15 and Regulation 2.1 require the record to be _accurate
and up to date_, so the honest blank is the compliant entry.

**Two roles, two records.** The Law separates Controller from Processor, and this Company is both,
for different data, at the same time. The published policy already says so in its own words
(`src/views/Legal.tsx:170`). Part 2 therefore labels each activity with the role it is carried out
in, and Part 1 §A states both identities. Article 15(2) requires a Processor to keep the same field
set for processing done on behalf of a Controller — that is why one document, role-labelled,
satisfies both duties rather than two.

### 3.2 The transfer question the record cannot answer yet

Articles 26 and 27 of the Law split transfers into "adequate level of protection" and "no adequacy,
so you need safeguards". Two facts make this live rather than theoretical:

1. The primary database sits in **Germany** (measured), and voice inference sits with vendors whose
   processing locations are currently undocumented — the personal data of Gulf bank customers leaves
   the DIFC to reach it.
2. The published policy already commits to an answer under the _European_ regime, not the DIFC one:
   "transfers outside the EEA are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision"
   (`src/views/Legal.tsx:184`). SCCs are an GDPR artefact. They are evidence of a safeguard, but the
   Commissioner's assessment under Articles 26/27 is a separate determination, and **no Article 26/27
   transfer assessment exists in this repository at all**.

`OPEN`, and it is a legal item rather than an engineering one: whether each recipient in §3.1 is
covered by an adequacy determination under Article 26, or requires Article 27 safeguards, and if so
which instrument is actually signed with that vendor. An unexecuted template in the repository is not
a safeguard.

| Redacted case row | 7 years                              | row deletion path `deleteCaseRecord`                                                   | same evidence bundle                                                                                                                                     |
| Audit chain       | never mutated or deleted             | refusal is a code path (`assertRetentionMutationAllowed`)                              | `src/lib/privacy/retention.ts:19-31`                                                                                                                     |

Deletion-by-key-destruction is the design's answer to the conflict every fraud product meets: an
append-only evidentiary log and a 90-day erasure deadline cannot both be satisfied by deleting rows.
Recorded so a reviewer sees the reasoning, not just the number.

## 7. High Risk Processing Activities — the determination that drives everything else

### 7.1 The definition, verbatim

Schedule 1 of the Law: "High Risk Processing" means processing where **one or more** of the following
applies —

> (a) Processing that includes the adoption of new or different technologies or methods, which creates
> a materially increased risk to the security or rights of a Data Subject or renders it more difficult
> for a Data Subject to exercise his rights;
> (b) a considerable amount of Personal Data will be Processed … and where such Processing is likely to
> result in a high risk to the Data Subject, including due to the sensitivity of the Personal Data …;
> (c) the Processing will involve a systematic and extensive evaluation of personal aspects relating to
> natural persons, based on automated Processing, including Profiling, and on which decisions are based
> that produce legal effects concerning the natural person or similarly significantly affect the natural
> person; or
> (d) a material amount of Special Categories of Personal Data is to be Processed.

Source: `difc-law.txt:2975-2994`, extracted with `pdftotext` from the official English PDF of DIFC
Law No. 5 of 2020 on 2026-10-18. (An earlier draft of this analysis relied on a model's _summary_ of
that PDF and invented paragraph numbers. It was wrong; the primary text was downloaded and the wording
above is transcribed from it. Nothing in this document cites a provision that has not been read.)

### 7.2 Applying it honestly

| Limb                                                                                         | Assessment                                                          | Reasoning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (a) new technology raising risk / making rights harder                                       | **Arguably yes**                                                    | An AI voice agent placing outbound calls that appear to come from a customer's bank is exactly the "new or different method" limb. Mitigating this is the product's own design (`policy.ts` item 1 forces the opening disclosure that it is an automated system calling on the bank's behalf), and the opt-out is honoured structurally (A5). Reduced, not eliminated.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| (b) considerable volume + sensitivity                                                        | **Not today; yes at scale**                                         | Live state: near-zero traffic and no bank deployment yet (project record `securevoice-integrations-live-state`). Limb (b) is a volume test, so it reads "not yet" — which is precisely the condition that changes without any code change on the first production pilot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| (c) systematic and extensive automated evaluation with legal / similarly significant effects | **The strongest candidate — but the decisive facts cut against it** | The platform _does_ systematically evaluate a named person's transaction behaviour, and a card restriction has real consequences. What saves the analysis is the architecture, and it is worth stating with evidence: the verdict comes from the caller's **own keypad press** (`src/lib/voice/dtmf.ts:147-154` maps `1` → `deny_fraud`, `2` → `confirm_authorized`), not from a model; the LLM is _told_ the already-verified intent and only words the reply, while the four commitment-bearing intents skip the model entirely and speak the vetted script verbatim (`src/lib/llm.ts:58-62`, `:590`) (`src/lib/llm.ts:724`); the freeze is only ever **staged** (`src/lib/ai/ai-authz.ts:76`) and "nothing is reversed: this platform never wrote a hold at the bank" (`:69`); a human commits it (`src/app/api/console/freeze/commit/route.ts:13`: "the agent stages `committed: false`, a human or bank system commits it"), and the operator's identity is written to the audit row (`:102`). Under Article 38(1) the right attaches to decisions based **solely** on automated processing; this design deliberately never reaches "solely". |
| (d) material amount of Special Categories                                                    | **Yes in kind, not yet in amount**                                  | `hearing_impaired` is health-related → Special Category (limb (d)). A boolean flag on a handful of cases is not a "material amount"; the moment accessibility routing is used to _exclude_ rather than to _accommodate_, or the flag is kept beyond the case, the calculus changes. See §7.3.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

**Working conclusion: limb (a) and limb (d) put this on the boundary, and limb (c) is the item a
reviewer will push hardest.** The prudent planning assumption — and the one this document adopts —
is that a bank deployment of this product **is** High Risk Processing. Everything below follows from
that assumption, and it is cheap to adopt because the mitigations are already in the code.

**The `hearing_impaired` field is the finding to fix in code.** Its purpose is accommodation: a
caller who reports hearing impairment gets SMS-only contact instead of a voice call
(`src/app/api/v1/interventions/route.ts:773-806`), and the state machine protects it — a case
reaching `UNREACHABLE` through that route must not be re-dialled, because "re-dialling that customer
is the accessibility failure the SMS-only path exists to avoid" (`src/lib/case-state-machine.ts:105-108`).
That is good engineering and bad records management, because:

- it is Special Category data (health) with **no Article 11 condition recorded**;
- it arrives from the bank over an authenticated webhook, so the bank is the collector — which makes
  us a Processor of special-category data under an instruction we do not store;
- it is retained with the case for 7 years to keep routing correct, long after the alert closes, and
  a disability flag that outlives its purpose is the textbook Art 22 failure;
- `src/lib/analytics.ts:104` counts it, so the derived population appears in tenant analytics.

Recommended remediation, in order of cost: (1) record the Art 11 condition on the intake contract and
require the bank's documented instruction to name it; (2) shorten the retention of that one field to
the life of the case rather than inheriting the 7-year tier; (3) keep the counting but stop storing
the flag once the channel decision is made.

### 7.4 Regulation 3 — notification to the Commissioner

Regulation 3.2 requires notifying the Commissioner of processing operations including special-category
processing and transfers outside the DIFC to recipients without an adequate level of protection, with
contents (a)-(h), **filed within 30 days of commencing the processing, renewed on each anniversary,
and re-filed within 30 days of processing differing from the initial notification** (Regulation 3,
time-for-filing rule).

Two `OPEN` items the owner must settle with counsel, because the repository cannot answer them:

- whether the small-entity carve-out in Regulation 3.1.1 (which is written against "Article 15 and
  Regulation 2") also excuses a **Regulation 3 notification**, or only the RoPA; and
- if notification is due, whether one was ever filed, and when it expires.

Filing the notification is largely a transcription exercise from this RoPA plus Part 3 §3.1. The
anniversary and the "changed processing" rules mean it must be re-filed before the first bank pilot,
not after.
